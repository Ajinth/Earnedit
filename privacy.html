Earnedit Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Earnedit helps you build healthier screen-time habits by connecting daily activity goals with app access.

Information Earnedit accesses

With your permission, Earnedit reads activity and workout information from Apple Health to calculate progress toward your goal. It also uses Apple’s Screen Time and Family Controls features to block the apps you choose.

How your information is used

Health and screen-time information is used on your device to provide Earnedit’s activity tracking and app-blocking features. Earnedit does not sell, advertise with, or share this information, and it does not send your Apple Health data to a server.

Purchases

If you buy a one-day break, Apple processes the payment through the App Store. Earnedit does not receive your payment card details.

Data retention and deletion

Earnedit does not require an account. App settings and local purchase state remain on your device and can be removed by deleting the app. Apple Health permissions can be changed at any time in the Health app.
