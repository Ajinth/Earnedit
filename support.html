Earnedit Support

Earnedit connects your daily activity goal to the apps you choose to protect.

Getting started

  1. Connect Apple Health and allow Earnedit to read activity.
  2. Connect Screen Time and choose the apps to protect.
  3. Set your daily activity goal and start blocking.

If activity is missing

Open Apple Health and confirm your activity has synced. Then check Health permissions for Earnedit in Health > Sharing > Apps. If you have been active today, allow a few minutes for Health to sync.

If app blocking is not working

Confirm Screen Time access is enabled, the correct apps are selected, and blocking is running. Restarting Earnedit can refresh permissions.

One-day break

The optional one-time purchase unlocks your selected apps until local midnight. Apple handles payment through the App Store.
